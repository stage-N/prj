Privacy Policy

STAGEN Inc. ("we", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information in Touten (the "Service").

Free features work without account registration. Payment details are processed via Stripe only when subscribing to Premium.

1. Information We Collect

  • Free Usage: We do not collect personally identifiable information such as name or email (no behavioral tracking cookies or ad SDKs).
  • Premium Subscription: Stripe processes email and payment details needed for billing. Our server (Cloudflare D1) stores only identifiers required to manage subscription state.
  • Access Logs: Cloudflare infrastructure logs (IP address, request timestamps, etc.) may be technically recorded.

2. External News Fetching & AI Processing

Touten fetches headlines and articles from public RSS feeds of Japan's five major national dailies and uses Google Gemini API for summarization and stance analysis. Fetched content is stored on our servers for analysis and display.

Your device never sends personal data to third-party media outlets you do not subscribe to; fetching is performed by our backend.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Stripe: Paid subscription billing and management
  • Google Gemini API: Article summarization and stance analysis
  • Cloudflare: Hosting, database (D1), and cache (KV)

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or provide user personal information to third parties except as required by law.

5. Contact

Please contact us via the Support page for privacy inquiries.

6. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated without prior notice and takes effect when posted on this page.
