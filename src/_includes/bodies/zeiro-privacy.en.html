Privacy Policy

STAGEN Inc. ("we", "our") sets forth this Privacy Policy regarding the handling of user privacy and business data in our integrated accounting and payroll service "Zeiro" (the "Service").

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Information: Email address, name, and password hashed using PBKDF2 (100,000 iterations).
  • Business Master Data: Company/trade name, fiscal year-end month, corporate number, Qualified Invoice Issuer number, and office address.
  • Accounting & Transaction Data: Chart of accounts, journal entries, bank/card transaction details, general ledger, and closing audit logs.
  • Director & Employee Payroll Data: Name, standard monthly remuneration grade, health/pension/employment insurance premiums, withholding tax, and payslip records.

2. Purpose of Use

  • Automated generation of double-entry journals, trial balances, and financial statements.
  • Accurate calculation of social insurance premiums and withholding income tax for directors and staff.
  • Deduplication of bank/card statements and AI journal entry inference.
  • Preparation of semi-annual withholding tax schedules and year-end closing health audits.
  • Subscription billing management and abuse prevention.

3. Payment Processing (Stripe)

Paid subscriptions (Pro / Business) are processed by Stripe, Inc. (PCI-DSS Level 1 certified). Credit card numbers never pass through or get stored on our servers.

4. Use of AI Features (Gemini API)

Zeiro uses Google Gemini API for journal inference. Submitted transaction context is used strictly to infer accounts and tax categories and is never used by Google to train or reuse AI models under API terms.

5. Data Storage & Security

The Service runs on Cloudflare (Workers / D1 / R2) with TLS 1.3 / HTTPS encryption in transit and strict tenant-ID isolation so corporate data is never mixed across tenants.

6. Disclosure, Correction & Deletion

Users can view, modify, or request deletion of their account and all associated corporate data at any time via account settings or support.

7. Policy Changes

We may update this policy as laws or features evolve and will announce material updates in the Service or on our website.

8. Contact

For privacy inquiries, please reach out via the Support page or Contact Form.
