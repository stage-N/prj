Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is StockPulse an investment advisory app?

No. This app provides informational analysis based on public market data only and is not investment advice or solicitation. Grades and reference levels are informational; please make investment decisions at your own responsibility.

Q. How often is market data updated?

Stock prices are fetched when you run an analysis. Institutional short-interest data may lag by 2–3 business days, and margin balances update weekly on Fridays.

Q. Push notifications are not arriving

Notifications and background monitoring are unavailable in Expo Go; a development/production build (EAS Build) is required. Also verify notification permissions in Settings.

Q. Is there a Web version?

Yes. Visit stockpulse.sta3e-n.online in your browser. Premium works with the same account as the mobile app.

Q. Where is my data stored?

On mobile, data is stored locally in on-device SQLite. See the Privacy Policy for details.
