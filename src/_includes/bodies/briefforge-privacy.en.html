Privacy Policy

STAGEN Inc. (“we”) respects your privacy when you use BriefForge (“the Service”).

BriefForge is a product-exploration tool. Sign in with GitHub; the same OAuth token is used to create Issues. Brief content may be sent to AI providers to generate hypotheses.

1. Information we collect

  • GitHub sign-in: user id, email, and display name stored in Cloudflare D1
  • Service data: briefs, hypotheses, signals, and decision logs
  • GitHub token: OAuth access token encrypted at rest, used to create Issues
  • Access logs: Cloudflare infrastructure logs (IP, timestamps) may be recorded

2. AI processing

Brief content may be sent to Workers AI or Gemini (or similar) for generation/reroll.

3. Third parties

  • GitHub: authentication and Issue export
  • Cloudflare: hosting, D1, Workers AI
  • Google Gemini (optional): hypothesis generation

4. Sharing

We do not sell personal data. We share only as required by law or to operate the providers above.
