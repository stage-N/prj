Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Does Touten endorse any political party or ideology?

No. Touten aims to neutrally visualize reporting tendencies across newspapers and does not endorse any political or economic position. Displays are reference information only.

Q. When is data updated?

Front-page articles from the five dailies are generally fetched and analyzed twice daily (morning and evening JST). Updates may occasionally be delayed.

Q. How do I cancel Premium?

You can cancel anytime via the Stripe Customer Portal from the in-app Premium menu or your subscription confirmation email.

Q. Are AI analyses always accurate?

Because analyses are generated automatically by Gemini, inaccuracies or nuances may occur. Please verify important decisions against primary sources.
