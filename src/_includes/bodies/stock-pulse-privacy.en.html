Privacy Policy

STAGEN Inc. ("we", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information in StockPulse (the "App").

This App is not investment advice or solicitation. Make all investment decisions at your own responsibility.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Registration (Optional): Email address and hashed password stored in Cloudflare D1.
  • Portfolio & Analysis (Mobile): Holdings, quantities, cost basis, analysis history, and settings are stored locally in on-device SQLite by default.
  • Portfolio & Analysis (Web): When signed in, holdings and analysis history sync to Cloudflare D1.
  • Premium Subscriptions: Subscription status is synced via Stripe, App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat using subscription identifiers and purchase history.
  • Device & App Identifiers: Push notification SDKs (Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging) or billing SDKs (RevenueCat / Google Play Billing) may collect device or app identifiers.
  • Access Logs: Infrastructure logs (IP address, request timestamps) may be recorded by Cloudflare; approximate city-level location may be inferred from IP.

2. External Market Data

Public market, financial, and short-interest data is fetched on user action from third-party sites (typically sending only ticker codes):

  • Kabutan (kabutan.jp)
  • Yahoo Finance
  • karauri.net

3. Notifications & Background Tasks

Risk alerts and daily summaries are scheduled on-device. Push delivery may use Expo, Apple, or Google (Firebase) infrastructure.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Stripe / RevenueCat / App Store / Google Play: Payments and subscriptions
  • Expo / Firebase Cloud Messaging: Push notifications
  • Cloudflare: Hosting, D1, and Workers

5. Data Deletion

To request deletion of your account or personal data, please contact us via the Support page.

6. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties except as required by law or to operate the service providers listed above.

7. Contact

For questions about this policy, please contact us via the Support page.

8. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated without prior notice and takes effect when posted on this page.
