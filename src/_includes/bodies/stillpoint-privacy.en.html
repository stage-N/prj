Privacy Policy

STAGEN Inc. ("we", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information in Stillpoint (the "Service").

This Service is a self-reflection aid and is not medical care or psychotherapy. In a crisis, please contact local support or emergency services.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Registration: Email address and hashed password stored in Cloudflare D1.
  • Session & Reflection Data: Daily prompts, AI questions, transcribed text reflections, and turn durations stored on our servers for sync.
  • Premium Subscription: Subscription state is synced via Stripe, App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat; we store subscription identifiers only.
  • Access Logs: Cloudflare infrastructure logs (IP address, request timestamps) may be technically recorded.

2. AI Processing

Transcribed reflection text may be sent via our backend to Google Gemini API (or Cloudflare Workers AI fallback) to generate deeper Socratic follow-up questions. Generated questions are saved as part of your session.

Speech-to-text runs on-device or via browser Web Speech API by default; we do not send raw audio recordings to third-party STT providers (depending on OS/browser implementation).

3. Data Retention (Free)

Free archives keep the most recent 7 days. Sessions past 7 days are both hidden and deleted from our servers. Premium retains archives indefinitely.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Stripe / RevenueCat / App Store / Google Play: Billing & subscriptions
  • Google Gemini API / Cloudflare Workers AI: Follow-up question generation
  • Cloudflare: Hosting, D1 database, and KV

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties except as required by law.

6. Contact

Please contact us via the Support page.

7. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated without prior notice and takes effect when posted on this page.
