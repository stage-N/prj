Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Is Stillpoint a substitute for medical care or counseling?

No. Stillpoint is a self-reflection aid. If you are experiencing a crisis or serious mental distress, please contact professional or emergency services.

Q. What is the difference between Free and Premium?

Free includes 2 AI deepening turns per session, a 7-day archive (deleted after expiry), and rotating domains. Premium offers unlimited turns and sessions, unlimited searchable archive, domain selection, and contrast cards.

Q. Where is my voice processed?

By default, speech recognition runs on-device or via browser Web Speech API; only transcribed text is sent to generate AI follow-up questions.

Q. Are AI questions always accurate?

AI-generated questions may reflect occasional misinterpretations. They are prompts for reflection rather than answers; rely on your own judgment and qualified professionals for important decisions.

Q. How do I cancel Premium?

Manage Web subscriptions via the Stripe Customer Portal and mobile subscriptions via App Store or Google Play settings.
