Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)

Q. What is Zeiro?

Zeiro is an integrated accounting and payroll SaaS tailored for Japanese 1-person corporations (micro-corporations), covering double-entry bookkeeping, zero-touch email transaction ingestion, Gemini AI journal inference, Kyokai Kenpo Tokyo payroll & withholding tax calculation, semi-annual withholding tax reports, and 9-point year-end closing audits.

Q. How does Zeiro differ from Money Forward Cloud or freee?

By eliminating enterprise bloat and focusing on what micro-corporations actually need, Zeiro offers a Pro plan at ¥9,360/year (¥780/month equivalent)—about 1/3 to 1/4 the cost of competitors—while including director payroll and withholding tax schedules out of the box.

Q. How are director compensation and social insurance calculated?

Zeiro calculates health insurance, employee pension, withholding income tax, and resident tax using Kyokai Kenpo (Tokyo) standard monthly grade tables and NTA monthly withholding tables, and posts the corresponding double-entry payroll journal in one click.

Q. How does zero-touch transaction email sync work?

When Zeiro receives bank or credit card alert emails (e.g., GMO Aozora, SBI Sumishin, SMBC, AMEX), it automatically parses amount, date, and counterparty and deduplicates via SHA-256 fingerprints.

Q. Can I migrate data from Money Forward?

Yes. Drag and drop your exported Money Forward journal CSV to import all historical entries losslessly.

Q. How does the withholding tax special schedule work?

For corporations with fewer than 10 employees on the semi-annual payment schedule (January and July), Zeiro automatically aggregates headcount, total payments, and withheld tax for the payment slip.

Q. Can I change or cancel my plan anytime?

Yes, directly via the Stripe Customer Portal with one click. Pro access remains active through the end of the billing period.

Q. Does Zeiro integrate with Rakubill?

Yes. Invoices issued in STAGEN's sister invoicing service Rakubill sync automatically with receivables so payment clearing journals can be generated in one click.
