Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Does Phrase Flow endorse a specific ideology or political stance?

No. Phrase Flow provides historical context and dual perspectives and does not promote any particular ideology. AI commentary and contrasting views are informational only.

Q. When are quotes updated?

All 9 categories are generated daily in early-morning KST batches and published to Web and email deliveries.

Q. What is the difference between Free and Premium?

Free includes 3 rotating categories per day, core background essays, and a 7-day archive. Premium unlocks all 9 categories, full Contrast View, up to 3 scheduled alerts/day, and unlimited archive.

Q. How do I cancel Premium?

Cancel anytime via the Stripe Customer Portal from the in-app Premium menu or your confirmation email.

Q. Is AI commentary always accurate?

Because explanations are AI-generated via Gemini, inaccuracies may occur. Verify critical claims with primary texts and multiple sources.
