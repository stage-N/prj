Frequently Asked Questions

Q. What is the difference between Free and Premium?

Free supports up to 3 active books and 30 days of recent logs. Premium unlocks unlimited library and history, completion predictions, CSV export, and multi-device sync.

Q. Are reminders pushed from your server?

No. Reminders use on-device local notifications and require notification permission on your device.

Q. What if an ISBN lookup returns no match?

You can register the book manually by entering its title and total page count; cover images may sync later.

Q. How do I cancel Premium?

Manage Web subscriptions via the Stripe Customer Portal and mobile subscriptions via App Store or Google Play settings.
