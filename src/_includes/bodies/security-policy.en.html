(hereinafter referred to as "the Company") is committed to protecting the information assets entrusted to us by our customers, business partners, and affiliated professionals, as well as the Company's own information assets across all applications, cloud systems, web services, and related businesses planned, developed, and operated by the Company, against threats such as accidents, disasters, and cybercrime. To earn and maintain the trust of our customers and society, we implement company-wide information security based on the following basic policy.

1. Management Responsibility

The Company strives to systematically and continuously improve and enhance information security under the direct leadership of top management.

2. Establishment of Internal Governance

The Company establishes an internal governance structure (Chief Information Security Officer) to maintain and improve information security and formalizes security measures as official company policies.

3. Employee and Partner Commitment

All officers, employees, and contractors of the Company acquire the knowledge and skills necessary for information security and ensure strict adherence to security practices.

4. Compliance with Laws and Contractual Obligations

The Company complies with the Act on the Protection of Personal Information (APPI), the My Number Act, the Certified Administrative Procedures Legal Specialist Act, the Telecommunications Business Act, and all other laws, regulations, standards, and contractual obligations related to information security, while meeting the expectations of our customers.

5. Incident Response and Prevention of Recurrence

In the event of any legal violation, breach of contract, or security incident related to information security, the Company responds promptly and appropriately and takes thorough measures to prevent recurrence.

■ IPA "SECURITY ACTION (Two-Star ★★)" & Company-Wide Security Architecture

STAGEN Inc. has formally declared "★★ Two-Star" under the "SECURITY ACTION" initiative established by the Information-technology Promotion Agency, Japan (IPA).

Based on the "Information Security Measures Guidelines for SMEs (Ver. 4.0)" established by the Information-technology Promotion Agency, Japan (IPA), the Company practices the "5 Information Security Commandments," conducts the "Information Security Self-Diagnosis," and publicly discloses this Information Security Basic Policy as a company-wide standard.

  • Local-First & Zero-Server PII Architecture: Across our mobile apps and business SaaS products (ZaiTap, ZeiCal, Rakubill, Zeiro, Heat Stroke Recorder Pro, etc.), we enforce least-privilege and on-device processing principles so unnecessary personal information is never retained on our servers.
  • End-to-End & Transport Encryption: All communications are protected via TLS 1.3, and sensitive document sharing utilizes client-side end-to-end encryption (PBKDF2 + AES-256-GCM) with automatic expiration.
  • PCI-DSS Compliant Payment Infrastructure: Payment processing is handled directly by PCI-DSS compliant providers (Stripe, Apple App Store, Google Play); credit card details are never stored on Company servers.
  • Access Control & Audit Logging: Multi-factor authentication (2FA/Passkeys), least-privilege access control, and tamper-evident audit logs are enforced across all development and cloud infrastructure.
