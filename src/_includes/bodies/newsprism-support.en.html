Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Does NewsPrism endorse any political party or ideology?

No. NewsPrism aims to neutrally visualize reporting tendencies across five US outlets and does not endorse any political or economic position. Displays are reference information only.

Q. When is data updated?

Top articles from the five outlets are generally fetched and analyzed twice daily (morning and evening US Eastern Time). Updates may occasionally be delayed.

Q. How do I cancel Premium?

You can cancel anytime via the Stripe Customer Portal from the in-app Premium menu or your subscription confirmation email.

Q. Are AI analyses always accurate?

Because analyses are generated automatically by Gemini, inaccuracies or nuances may occur. Please verify important decisions against primary sources.

Q. How does NewsPrism differ from Touten and Banjem?

Touten covers five major Japanese newspapers, Banjem covers five South Korean outlets, and NewsPrism covers five major US outlets in the same STAGEN media literacy lineup.
