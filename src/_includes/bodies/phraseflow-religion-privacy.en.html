Privacy Policy

STAGEN Inc. ("we", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information in Daily Sacred Verse (the "Service").

Free reading requires no account. Payment details are processed via Stripe only when subscribing to Premium.

1. Information We Collect

  • Free Usage: No name or email is required to read passages.
  • Account Registration: Email address and hashed password stored in Cloudflare D1.
  • Premium: Stripe processes payment details; we store subscription identifiers only.
  • Access Logs: Cloudflare infrastructure logs may be technically recorded.

2. AI Processing & Content

Context, commentary, and comparison texts are generated via Google Gemini API and stored in Cloudflare D1. Your device never sends personally identifiable data directly to Gemini.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Stripe: Paid subscription billing
  • Google Gemini API: Generating context, commentary, and comparisons
  • Cloudflare: Hosting, D1, and KV

4. Contact

Please contact us via the Support page.
