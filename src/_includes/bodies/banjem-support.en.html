Frequently Asked Questions

Q. Does Banjem endorse any political party or ideology?

No. Banjem aims to neutrally visualize reporting tendencies across five Korean outlets and does not endorse any political or economic position. Displays are reference information only.

Q. When is data updated?

Top articles from the five outlets are generally fetched and analyzed twice daily (morning and evening KST). Updates may occasionally be delayed.

Q. How do I cancel Premium?

You can cancel anytime via the Stripe Customer Portal from the in-app Premium menu or your subscription confirmation email.

Q. Are AI analyses always accurate?

Because analyses are generated automatically by Gemini, inaccuracies or nuances may occur. Please verify important decisions against primary sources.

Q. How does Banjem differ from Touten?

Touten covers five major Japanese newspapers, while Banjem is its Korean-edition sister service covering five major South Korean outlets.
