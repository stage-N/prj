Privacy Policy

STAGEN Inc. ("we", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information in PagePace (the "Service").

PagePace is a reading habit tracking tool. Third-party book metadata services may be queried by ISBN for titles and covers.

1. Information We Collect

  • Account Registration: Email address and hashed password stored in Cloudflare D1.
  • Reading Data: Library entries (ISBN, title, page progress), session durations, and segment pace stored on our servers for sync.
  • Local Notifications: Daily reading reminders are scheduled on-device without server push.
  • Premium Subscription: Subscription state is synced via Stripe, App Store, Google Play, and RevenueCat; we store subscription identifiers only.
  • Access Logs: Cloudflare infrastructure logs (IP address, request timestamps) may be technically recorded.

2. Book Metadata Lookups

When looking up ISBN details or cover art, submitted ISBNs may be sent to external book APIs such as openBD, Rakuten Books, Open Library, or Google Books.

3. Data Retention (Free)

Free tier focuses on the most recent 30 days of logs; Premium retains full reading history indefinitely. Contact Support to request account deletion.

4. Third-Party Services

  • Stripe / RevenueCat / App Store / Google Play: Billing & subscriptions
  • openBD / Rakuten / Open Library / Google Books: Book metadata & cover images
  • Cloudflare: Hosting, D1 database, and KV

5. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or share personal information with third parties except as required by law.

6. Contact

Please contact us via the Support page.

7. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated without prior notice and takes effect when posted on this page.
