Privacy Policy

STAGEN Inc. ("we", "our") respects your privacy and is committed to protecting personal information in Phrase Flow (the "Service").

Free features work without account registration. Payment details are processed via Stripe only when subscribing to Premium.

1. Information We Collect

  • Free Usage: No name or email is required to read quotes (no behavioral tracking ad SDKs).
  • Account Registration: Email address and hashed password stored in Cloudflare D1.
  • Premium Subscription: Stripe processes payment details needed for billing; our servers store only identifiers needed to manage subscription state.
  • Email Delivery (Opt-in): Delivery email address and preference settings.
  • Access Logs: Cloudflare infrastructure logs (IP address, request timestamps) may be technically recorded.

2. AI Processing & Quote Content

Curated quote seeds are expanded into historical context, commentary, and dual perspectives via Google Gemini API in multilingual templates and stored in Cloudflare D1. Your device never sends personally identifiable data directly to Gemini.

3. Third-Party Services

  • Stripe: Paid subscription billing and management
  • Google Gemini API: Generating quote context, commentary, and contrast views
  • Cloudflare: Hosting, D1 database, and KV sessions
  • RevenueCat (Mobile): Mobile subscription state sync

4. Third-Party Sharing

We do not sell or provide personal information to third parties except as required by law.

5. Contact

Please contact us via the Support page.

6. Policy Changes

This policy may be updated without prior notice and takes effect when posted on this page.
